Adjustment Guidelines
Recommended process for warehouse stock correction.
1. Verify
Physically count the product in the selected godown before creating an adjustment.
2. Compare
Compare the physical quantity with the quantity shown in the ERP system.
3. Record
Enter the correct reason and remarks so the adjustment can be audited later.
